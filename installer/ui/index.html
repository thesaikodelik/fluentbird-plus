A calm, modern theme that follows your Windows accent colour, plus optional add-ons that make email faster.

Ready to uninstall

Thunderbird goes back to exactly how it was before Fluentbird+. If there is no record of that, it goes back to Thunderbird's own defaults.

  • ThemeRemoves the theme files and puts back your own, if you had any.
  • SettingsRestores user.js and the theme settings to their earlier values.

Finish adding your extras in Thunderbird
  1. Open Thunderbird and go to Tools › Add-ons and Themes
    Don't see Tools? Press Alt to show the menu bar.
  2. Search for the name below and click Add to Thunderbird, then Add.
• Colours follow Settings › Personalisation › Colours › Accent colour.
• Your new add-ons switch on the next time Thunderbird opens.
• Run this installer again any time to update or uninstall.
